THE FRONT OFFICE

Esports Company Management · Manager X

    📱 Turn log

    What happened as each quarter played out.

    📖 Words to know

    Cash reserves
    Your available money. You start with $250,000. Below $0 for two turns in a row means bankruptcy.
    Salaries
    Fixed costs paid every turn. They start at $30,000 and some hires raise them for good.
    TSR (Team Skill Rating)
    How good your players are, 0 to 100. Higher TSR means better win odds and more sponsor money.
    Morale
    How happy your players are, 0 to 100. It multiplies your win odds. At 0 the players quit and the game ends.
    Fans
    Your fanbase. Fans are required for promotion (7,000 for Tier 2, 12,000 for Tier 1).
    Rank (Tier)
    Tier 3 Rookie ($20K prize) → Tier 2 Challenger ($35K) → Tier 1 Elite ($60K).
    Tournaments
    One every turn. A win earns prize money and +1,500 fans. A loss costs a $10,000 sponsor penalty and 5% of your fans.
    Win probability
    (TSR × Morale%) ÷ 60, kept between 5% and 95%.
    Communications director
    A required hire (by Year 1, Quarter 3), and a fixed cost you have to carry. It costs $12,000 plus $6,000 a turn. It adds 300 fans and $2,500 in sponsor money every turn, stops PR incidents, and boosts channel campaigns by 25%. In cash alone it roughly breaks even, so it works like insurance.
    PR incident
    Without a communications director, each turn from Quarter 2 has a 40% chance of a public slip-up that costs $8,000 and 4% of your fans.
    Fixed costs
    Costs that repeat every quarter whether you win or lose. Here that is payroll (salaries).
    Variable costs
    Costs you choose each quarter: operations, marketing and player-focus spending, plus travel, loss penalties and PR incidents.
    Contribution margin
    Revenue minus variable costs. It has to be big enough to cover your fixed costs before you make a profit.
    Operating profit
    Contribution margin minus fixed costs. It is what the quarter added to (or took from) your cash.
    Break-even revenue
    Fixed plus variable costs: the revenue you had to earn just to stay level.
    Margin of safety
    How far revenue is above (or below) break-even. A small margin means one bad tournament can put you in the red.
    Runway
    How many quarters your cash lasts if you keep losing money at the current rate.
    Payback period
    How many quarters of extra income it takes to earn back what a choice costs. The estimate counts sponsor money and win odds only, using what a TSR or morale point is worth right now.
    Cash timing
    Money from deals you have signed arrives next quarter. You pay now and collect later, so profit and cash can tell different stories for a quarter.
